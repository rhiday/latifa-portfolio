About

A little about me

I'm Latifa — an architect by training, now drawn to the quiet question behind every immersive project: how should this feel to be inside of?

Over two years of practice in Riyadh and Dhaka taught me how space, light, and material come together in something people actually live and work in — from concept and material estimation to site delivery. I'm now studying XR design at Metropolia University of Applied Sciences, carrying that spatial sensibility into extended reality.

A multicultural background — Bangladesh, Saudi Arabia, Finland — shapes how I design: comfortable moving between perspectives, and looking for the details that make an experience legible to people whatever their starting point.
